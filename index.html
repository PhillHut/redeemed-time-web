How Redeemed Time Works

Designed to Transform Habits, Not Just Block Apps

Most blockers fail because you simply tap "ignore" or bypass them when willpower fades. Redeemed Time replaces empty distraction with spiritual clarity.

Intervention Engine

The 'Swap' Interceptor & Anti-Doomscroll Guard

When you reach for an addictive app, Redeemed Time introduces intentional, mindful friction before the algorithm takes hold. Choose between a peaceful countdown or strict sanctification prayer meditation.

Uncompromising Purity

Fortress-Level Guard Against Explicit Content

Redeemed Time operates a built-in local DNS firewall and keyword inspector that stops adult content at the device level before it ever loads into your browser or app feed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about setup, permissions, and accountability.

How does the Swap Interceptor actually help me stop doomscrolling? ▼
Most app opens are subconscious muscle-memory twitch reactions. By injecting a deliberate 10–20 second pause with Scripture and an alternative option ("Swap or Continue"), Redeemed Time interrupts the dopamine loop and gives your prefrontal cortex time to make a conscious, spiritual decision.
Does Redeemed Time track or record my browsing history? ▼
No. Redeemed Time was intentionally built to operate 100% locally on your smartphone. We do not maintain any external browsing history databases, we do not sell data to advertisers, and your personal app usage stays strictly on your physical device.
How do automated email alerts to accountability partners work? ▼
When you configure an accountability partner, emergency alerts are dispatched via secure cloud email (Amazon SES) only when critical events happen—such as a security shield breach, unauthorized app uninstall attempt, or when you explicitly activate the Panic Shield. Normal, private app usage is never broadcast.
What platforms does Redeemed Time support? ▼
Redeemed Time is built natively for Android, utilizing modern Jetpack Compose, local on-device accessibility guard services, and local DNS filtering for maximum battery efficiency and total user privacy.
Can I customize what apps or websites are shielded? ▼
Yes! In the settings menu, you have complete granular control over which apps have the Swap Interceptor applied, which websites are blocked, which words trigger real-time search shields, and an explicit whitelist for trusted Christian resources and work tools.

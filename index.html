How Redeemed Time Works

Less Scrolling. More of the Word. More Time Together.

Blocking alone rarely changes a habit. Redeemed Time gently interrupts the scroll, then points you somewhere better: Scripture, prayer, and the people around you.

Bible tab

Read the Word: the Whole Bible, Right in the App

Every book of the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) is built in and works without internet. Pick up where you left off, change the text size, search, or have a chapter read aloud.

  • All 66 books, offline: no internet or account needed.
  • Listen: have Scripture read aloud with your phone’s built-in voices, at the speed you like.
  • Your Bible app, if you prefer: open YouVersion, Dwell, Logos or Blue Letter Bible instead.
Today tab

See What Your Scrolling Time Could Become

The Today page shows how long you spent in video and social apps, and how much of God’s Word you could read in that same time. Not to shame you, just to help you choose.

  • Honest numbers: based on real Bible reading times, never more than the time you actually spent.
  • Other ideas: read a chapter aloud with your family, call a friend, or thank God for three things from today.
  • One tap to read: jump straight into the Bible from the card.
Today tab: check-ins

Meet, Be Honest, and Pray for Each Other

Real change happens in the light, with people who love you. Redeemed Time helps you keep a regular check-in with an accountability partner and come ready to talk.

  • Check-in schedule: weekly, every other week or monthly, added to your calendar so you both remember.
  • Private prep notes: jot down victories, HALT moments (hungry, angry, lonely, tired) and prayer needs. They stay on your phone.
  • A discussion guide: a simple outline for your time together, from thanksgiving to prayer.
  • Openness, not spying: once your partner accepts an invite, they get a short email for a few set events, for example if you turn a protection off or allow a website. Nothing else is shared.
Swap tab

A Pause Before Distracting Apps Open

Choose the apps that pull you in. When you open one, Redeemed Time shows a short pause with a verse, so you can choose: open the Word, pray, or continue on purpose.

  • You set the pause: anywhere from 5 seconds to 2 minutes.
  • Prayer pause (called “Strict Sanctification” in the app for now): an optional short guided prayer before the app opens.
  • Long-scroll reminder (“Anti-Doomscroll Reminder” in the app for now): a gentle check-in after a stretch of time in one app. You pick how long.
Shield tab

Guard Your Eyes: Adult Site Blocking and a FLEE Button

Redeemed Time checks the sites and searches you open in supported browsers, right on your phone, and covers explicit pages with a calm pause screen. When temptation hits, the FLEE button is one tap away on every screen.

  • Adult Site Blocker: blocks well-known adult sites, adult-only web addresses (like .xxx), and any site you add, in Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Opera and DuckDuckGo.
  • FLEE button (for moments of temptation): Scripture, a calming breathing prayer, and one tap to text or call your partner.
  • Victory streak: count your days walking in freedom.
Sanctuary tab

A Daily Verse and a Quiet Place to Pray

Get a verse each day as a notification, keep the verses that carry you, and pray through short guided prayers when you need to reset.

  • Daily verse: a notification with a verse or quote at the time you choose.
  • Guided prayers: including a 30-second reset and a prayer for when temptation strikes.
  • Your verses and quotes: save the passages that speak to you.
Everyday Life

Time Back for God and for People

A verse before the feed, a pause before the scroll, and an honest conversation each week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? Here Are Honest Answers.

What the app does, what it doesn’t, and how accountability works.

How does the pause help me stop scrolling? ▼
Many app opens happen on autopilot. When you open an app you’ve chosen, Redeemed Time shows a short pause (you pick 5 seconds to 2 minutes) with a verse and a few choices: read, pray, or continue. That small moment is often enough to choose differently.
Which Bible translation does it use? ▼
The Berean Standard Bible (BSB), all 66 books, built into the app and readable offline. Every verse the app quotes is checked word for word against it. If you prefer, the app can open YouVersion, Dwell, Logos or Blue Letter Bible instead.
Does Redeemed Time track or upload my browsing? ▼
No. The site blocker checks web addresses and searches on your phone, and nothing you browse is sent anywhere. It uses Android’s Accessibility setting, which lets the app see which app or website is on your screen. There are no ads or analytics.
How do partner emails work? ▼
You invite a partner by email, and they accept first. After that, they get a short, kind email only when you turn a protection off, allow a website, remove a blocked word or site, ask for extra time, open settings to pause the app, or erase the app’s data. The app tells you on screen before anything is sent. The FLEE button never emails anyone; from there, you choose whether to text or call your partner.
Which phones does it work on? ▼
Android phones, as a test version on Google Play for now. The site blocker works in Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Opera and DuckDuckGo.
Is there an iPhone version? ▼
Not yet. We’re starting with Android. If you’d like to hear when an iPhone version is ready, email support@redeemedtime.com.
Can I choose which apps and sites are covered? ▼
Yes. You choose which apps get the pause, and you can add your own blocked words and sites. You can also allow trusted sites; if you have a partner, they’re emailed the name of any site you allow.

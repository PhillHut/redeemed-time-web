Privacy Policy for Redeemed Time

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 • Version 2.1

Thank you for using Redeemed Time ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and treating your attention, data, and spiritual journey with the highest level of respect and integrity. This Privacy Policy outlines how our app handles device data, permissions, and user information.

Core Privacy Promise: Redeemed Time is designed with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. We do not sell your personal data, track your personal web browsing history to remote servers, or monitor keystrokes. Your screen time stats and prayer notes remain private on your device.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Redeemed Time processes minimal data locally on your device to deliver our spiritual stewardship and focus features:

  • On-Device App Usage Statistics: With your permission, the App reads daily foreground usage time from your device's usage stats system to calculate your time perspective (e.g., equivalent Bible books read). This data is processed strictly locally on your device.
  • Accountability Partner Information: Names, email addresses, and phone numbers that you manually enter for accountability purposes are saved in your device's local database. If you invite a partner to receive alerts, their email address, your first name and a random ID for your install are also stored by our alert service (see section E), only so it can send them alerts. Nothing else is stored there.
  • Custom Scriptures and Prayers: Any personal scriptures, reflections, or custom prayers you save are stored locally within the app's secure database.

2. Sensitive Permissions & Specific Declarations

To provide intentional friction against distractions and protect your spiritual integrity, Redeemed Time utilizes specific Android system permissions:

A. Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Purpose & Justification: Redeemed Time uses the Android Accessibility Service API solely to provide its core digital wellbeing and distraction-blocking features ("The Swap Interceptor" and "Purity Shield"):

  • It inspects the foreground package name to detect when a user-selected distraction app (e.g., social media or video streaming) is launched, prompting the user with a sacred pause, prayer, or Berean Standard Bible Scripture before the app unlocks.
  • It inspects browser address bars locally in real-time solely to detect explicit adult domain URLs or lust-triggering search phrases, immediately displaying the emergency "Flee Temptation" shield.
  • It recognizes when Redeemed Time’s own App Info page, its Accessibility switch, or an uninstall prompt for Redeemed Time is on screen, so it can show a pause before the app is stopped or removed (Tamper Protection).

Privacy Guarantee: The Accessibility Service does not log keystrokes, does not read personal text messages, does not capture screen recordings, and does not transmit any browsing or app data to external servers or third parties. The one exception is a choice you make: if you allow a website, its name is emailed to your accepted partners (see section E).

B. Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

Used strictly on-device to query how many minutes were spent on video and social media apps, in order to display the perspective shift (how much of the Bible could have been read in that same time). This information never leaves your phone.

C. Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Allows the App to display the sacred friction countdown overlay and the emergency Panic Shield when a blocked application or website is detected.

D. Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

Enables you to browse and select installed apps on your device that you wish to add to your personal Intercepted Apps list.

E. Automated Email Notification System

When you invite an accountability partner, our alert service (hosted on Amazon Web Services) emails them an invitation. Alerts go only to partners who accept, and only for these events: you open settings to pause the app, you turn a protection off, you allow a website (the site name is included), you remove a blocked word or site (the word or site itself is not included), you ask for extra time, you erase the app’s data, or you remove that partner. The app sends only a random install ID, the event and those details, over HTTPS. It never sends your browsing, searches or app usage. Partners can stop alerts with the link in every email. Partner email addresses are never sold, rented, or used for advertising.

F. Manual Messaging Intent

When you choose to manually contact a partner, the App initiates an Intent (`ACTION_SENDTO`) directing your phone's default messaging application to compose a pre-formatted message. Messages are never sent without your direct action.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. Partner alert emails are sent through our alert service on Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES), solely to deliver the alerts described above.

4. Data Retention and Security

All user preferences, custom prayers, accountability partner contacts, and logs are stored locally in the App’s private storage on your device, which Android encrypts when your phone uses a screen lock. If you uninstall the app or clear app data in Android Settings, all stored data is permanently deleted from the device.

5. Children's Privacy

Redeemed Time is designed for general audiences and individuals seeking spiritual discipline (recommended for users aged 13 and above, or 18+ for adult purity filtering). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

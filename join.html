How to join the test

Redeemed Time is being tested on Android before it's released to everyone. Joining takes about 2 minutes, and here's every step with pictures.

Android phones only About 2 minutes Free
1

Join the testing group

Google only lets members of our testing group install the test version. Open the group and tap Join group.

Open the Testing Group
⏳ Important Note: Please wait 3 to 5 minutes after joining!
Google takes a few minutes to sync new group members with the Play Store. If you jump to Step 2 immediately, Google might show “App not available”. Give it 3–5 minutes to register, and you’ll be all set.

Use the same Google account that's on your phone. If you have more than one Google account, check the account shown in the top corner of the group page before you tap Join.

2

Become a tester

Open the Google Play test page and tap the blue Become a tester button.

Open the Google Play test page

Seeing “App not available”? Don’t worry—you didn’t do anything wrong! Google takes 3 to 5 minutes to update after you join the group. Wait a couple of minutes, make sure you’re signed into the same Google account used in Step 1, and refresh the link.

3

Download it on Google Play

The page now says You are a tester. Tap download it on Google Play, then tap Install in the Play Store.

No Install button yet? It can take a little while after you become a tester. Open the Play Store app, search for Redeemed Time: Scripture Focus, or try the link again in a few minutes.

4

Open it and set it up

The app walks you through setup. A few things it will ask for, and why:

  • Usage access, to show how long you spent in the apps you choose.
  • Accessibility, so it can pause the apps you pick and block adult sites in your browser. The app explains this before you turn it on, and the checking happens on your phone.
  • An accountability partner is optional. If you invite someone, they get an email only after they accept, and only for a few set events.
5

Stay for 14 days and tell us what you think

Google needs testers to stay in the test for 14 days in a row before the app can be released to everyone. If you leave the test early, Google stops counting you, so please keep it installed.

Tell us anything that's confusing, broken, or could be kinder or clearer: reply to the invite you received, or email support@redeemedtime.com.
